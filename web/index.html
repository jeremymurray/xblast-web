XBlast TNT

A browser port of the classic Bomberman-style blast game. GPL-2.0-or-later, peer-to-peer multiplayer, no game server.

Solo

Play against the computer or share the keyboard locally.

Multiplayer

Share one room code. The host runs the game; traffic goes directly between browsers over WebRTC. Keep the host tab in the foreground; background tabs can pause the game.

Advanced: TURN

Only needed if you cannot connect. Use a relay you trust. Hosting your own copy with TURN is the recommended fix.

Menus
Tab / arrows to move, Space or Return to select
Left player
W A S D to move, Space to bomb, Shift to stop, Tab to kick
Right player
Arrow keys to move, Return to bomb
During a match
Esc leaves the match

The first click loads the game (about 20 MB) and enables sound.
