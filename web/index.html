XBlast TNT

A browser port of the classic Bomberman-style blast game. GPL-2.0-or-later, peer-to-peer multiplayer planned, no game server.

Menus
Tab / arrows to move, Space or Return to select
Left player
A S D F to move, Space to bomb, Shift to stop, Tab to kick
Right player
Arrow keys to move, Return to bomb
During a match
Esc leaves the match

Click Play to load the game (about 20 MB) and enable sound.

Thanks for playing XBlast TNT.
