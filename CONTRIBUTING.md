# Contributing

This is a **fun side project**, shared because it is fun to play with — not a
maintained product. **Contributions and issues are not being accepted at this
time.** Please do not open issues or pull requests.

You are welcome to **fork** the project and take it further under the terms of
GPL-2.0-or-later. If you publish a fork:

- keep `COPYING`, `THIRD_PARTY.md`, `LICENSE/` and the upstream notices intact;
- keep the AI-assistance disclosure (see `README.md` and the `Assisted-by:`
  trailers) rather than hiding how the port was made;
- do not imply that the original project supports or endorses the fork.

Security problems: see `SECURITY.md` rather than posting details publicly.

## Attribution policy

The maintainer's commits carry a DCO `Signed-off-by:` and, when AI-assisted, an
`Assisted-by:` trailer naming the model and provider. AI involvement is disclosed,
not hidden. A fork should keep that disclosure and the licence notices intact.

If contributions are ever opened, this file will state the licence terms
(inbound = outbound GPL-2.0-or-later), the DCO sign-off requirement and the AI
disclosure rule before any outside change is merged.
